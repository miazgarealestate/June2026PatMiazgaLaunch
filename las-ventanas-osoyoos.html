Community Spotlight — Las Ventanas Al Paraiso, Osoyoos BC

Windows to Paradise.
Only 11 of them.

An exclusive 11-unit beachfront condo building at 2418 Lakeshore Drive — geothermal heating and cooling, underground secure parking, a private dock and sandy beach. Turnover is rare.

Las Ventanas Al Paraiso — Spanish for "Windows to Paradise" — is a small, four-storey Santa Fe-styled condo building right on the water in Osoyoos. With only 11 units total, listings here are genuinely uncommon; when one does come up, it tends to be a 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom unit with lake and mountain views, geothermal heating and cooling built into the strata fee, and shared access to a private sandy beach and boat dock.

Who Las Ventanas Is For

Buyers who want genuine exclusivity and don't need to move fast

With only 11 units in the entire building, Las Ventanas doesn't behave like a typical resort strata — there's no steady stream of listings to compare against, and buyers who want in should expect to wait for the right unit rather than shop a large inventory. That scarcity is part of the appeal for people specifically drawn to this stretch of Lakeshore Drive.

Units are geothermal-heated and -cooled, with the system's operating cost built into the strata fee rather than billed separately — a detail worth understanding since it changes how you evaluate "monthly cost" compared to a conventionally heated condo. Recent units have shown high-end finishes: granite or quartz countertops, engineered or Brazilian cherrywood flooring, 9-foot ceilings, and gas fireplaces.

Because the building is small, due diligence should focus specifically on this strata's reserve fund and depreciation report rather than assuming it resembles larger, newer developments nearby.

What Buyers Need to Know

Buying at Las Ventanas Al Paraiso — the important details

📄 Strata Documents

Review bylaws, minutes, depreciation report and reserve fund status — especially important in an 11-unit building where a large repair bill is split fewer ways.

🌡️ Geothermal System

Ask what the strata fee covers for the geothermal heating/cooling system and who's responsible for the loop maintenance versus the in-unit equipment.

⛴️ Dock & Beach Access

Confirm current dock/moorage arrangements and any rules around the private sandy beach shared by the 11 units.

🅿️ Underground Parking

Confirm which parking stall(s) and storage locker are assigned to the unit, and the condition of the elevator and shared underground garage.

💰 Total Cost of Ownership

Strata fees, property tax, insurance — and since geothermal + gas fireplace are often bundled into the fee, compare total monthly cost rather than the fee alone.
