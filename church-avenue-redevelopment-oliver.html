Community Spotlight — Church Avenue Redevelopment Sites, Oliver BC

Two parcels, one redevelopment story.
Downtown Oliver.

Two adjoining downtown Oliver parcels offered for redevelopment: 511 Church (the former United Church, $725,000) and 545 Church ($200,000), or both together ($925,000), per MLS® listings.

According to MLS® listings, 511 Church Avenue is zoned Town Centre (TC) with plans to convert the existing United Church building to eight rental units (six one-bedroom, two two-bedroom), and 545 Church Avenue is zoned RH2 High Density Residential with plans for multi-level townhomes. The listings also say the OCP and zoning changes are in place and an easement requires five parking spaces at 545 for 511. These are listing statements; verify zoning, approvals and easements with the Town of Oliver before relying on them.
