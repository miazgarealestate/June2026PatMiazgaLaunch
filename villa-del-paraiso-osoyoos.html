Who Villa del Paraiso Is For

Two different kinds of buyer

Residential buyers are drawn to the walkable location directly across from the beach, near shops, a bakery, golf and wineries. One listing described a top-floor end-unit penthouse with panoramic lake views, three bedrooms and two bathrooms.

Commercial buyers or tenants get lakeside street frontage on a visible corner. Neighbouring businesses mentioned in MLS® remarks include a bakery, a property-management office, cleaning services and massage therapy.

Mixed-use strata have their own considerations — shared costs between residential and commercial owners, use restrictions, and different insurance and financing treatment. Review the bylaws and any commercial/residential cost-sharing in the strata documents.

What Buyers Need to Know

Buying at Villa del Paraiso — the important details

Read the bylaws and minutes closely for residential versus commercial cost allocation and use restrictions.

🏪 Commercial Use

If you are buying commercial space, confirm zoning, permitted uses, signage rules and tenant arrangements.

🏠 Residential Use

Confirm pet, rental and short-term rental rules for residential units; they may differ from commercial rules.

Mixed-use buildings can be treated differently by lenders and insurers.

Built in the mid-2000s. Pat's construction background helps assess envelope and mechanical systems.

Fees, property tax, insurance and special levies — confirm what is included.

📄 BC Depreciation Report Rule

Since July 1, 2024, a strata corporation with five or more lots (bare land stratas included) can no longer defer a depreciation report by a ¾ vote. Outside Metro Vancouver, the Fraser Valley and the Capital Regional District, a strata with no report, or only one dated before December 31, 2020, has until July 1, 2027 to obtain one.
