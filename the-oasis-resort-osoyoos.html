Community Spotlight — The Oasis Resort, Osoyoos BC

Eight acres.
105 deeded sites.

A fully serviced, deeded RV community on 8 acres of Osoyoos Lake frontage — heated pool, fitness centre, and a private beach, with every site individually owned rather than leased.

The Oasis Resort is one of the larger deeded RV developments on Osoyoos Lake, with 105 individually owned, fully serviced sites spread across 8 lakefront acres. "Deeded" ownership here means buyers hold real title to their site — the same underlying legal footing as any other titled BC property, distinct from the pad-lease or long-term site-lease models seen at some other RV resorts in the area.

Who The Oasis Resort Is For

Buyers who want real title on a genuine lakefront RV site

The Oasis Resort appeals to RV owners who want the clearest possible legal footing for their purchase: deeded title to a fully serviced site rather than a pad lease or a fractional interest. At 8 acres, it's a larger footprint than many comparable RV developments in Osoyoos, giving it room for a full amenity package — heated pool, fitness centre, and private beach — without feeling cramped.

Because every one of the 105 sites is individually owned, resale activity here functions much like any other strata or titled real estate market, with comparable sales to reference rather than relying purely on developer pricing.

As with any RV-lot purchase, the appeal is largely about lifestyle and seasonal use rather than permanent residence — but the deeded structure gives owners a genuinely different asset than a leasehold or membership-style RV community.

What Buyers Need to Know

Buying at The Oasis Resort — the important details

📄 New BC Depreciation Report Rule

As of 2024, deferring a depreciation report by vote is no longer allowed — South Okanagan strata corporations (including bare land strata) have until July 1, 2027 to be compliant. See the full guide →

📄 Title & Strata Documents

Confirm the exact form of deeded ownership (bare land strata versus another structure), and review bylaws, minutes and reserve fund status.

🔌 Service Hookups

Verify what "fully serviced" includes for the specific site — power amperage, water, sewer — and any recent infrastructure upgrades or planned assessments.

🚐 RV Requirements

Confirm any size, age, or condition rules for RVs on site, and whether park models or permanent structures are permitted.

💰 Total Cost of Ownership

Strata or maintenance fees, property tax, and insurance. Ask what the fee structure covers versus what's separately billed.

📆 Seasonal Use Rules

Confirm any restrictions on winter occupancy or minimum/maximum stay periods if that affects your plans.
