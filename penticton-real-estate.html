Community Spotlight — Penticton, BC

The city between two lakes.
The South Okanagan's largest market.

Penticton sits between Okanagan Lake and Skaha Lake and is the largest real estate market in the South Okanagan, with about 570 active listings in the October 9, 2026 MLS® export.

Pat's core market is Osoyoos and the South Okanagan, and Penticton matters to many of his buyers as the regional hub and a more urban alternative. This page summarizes what is listed across Penticton's MLS® sub-areas and points to nearby communities such as Okanagan Falls and Naramata.

Why Penticton

For buyers who want city services and lake access

Penticton suits buyers who want more services, restaurants, health care and amenities than Osoyoos or Oliver offer, with two lakes close by.

The MLS® export splits Penticton into sub-areas such as Main North, Main South, Columbia/Duncan, Wiltse/Valleyview, Uplands/Redlands, Apex, Industrial, Husula/West Bench/Sage Mesa and Penticton Rural. Sub-areas can feel very different; look at them individually.

Many Osoyoos-area buyers compare Penticton on price, services and distance. Pat lives in the Osoyoos area and can help with that comparison.

📋 Penticton at a glance

  • 📍Location: Between Okanagan Lake and Skaha Lake
  • 🏙️Sub-areas in MLS® export: Main North, Main South, Columbia/Duncan, Wiltse/Valleyview, Uplands/Redlands, Apex, Industrial, Rural, Husula/West Bench/Sage Mesa
  • 🏠Home types listed: Detached homes, apartments, townhomes, half-duplexes, lots, manufactured homes and more
  • 📏Inventory: About 570 active listings in the Oct 9, 2026 export
  • 🚗Distance: Penticton is the regional hub for much of the South Okanagan
What Buyers Need to Know

Buying in Penticton — the important details

🏢 Strata Purchases

Apartments and townhomes make up a large share of listings. Review strata documents, depreciation reports and bylaws.

🔥 Wildfire & Insurance

Ask your insurer early about coverage for hillside and interface properties.

🌊 Lake & Waterfront

Waterfront and lake-access properties carry specific rules, setbacks and insurance considerations.

📜 Rentals & Short-Term Rental Rules

Rules differ by municipality and strata. Confirm before assuming rental income.

🏗️ Older Homes

Penticton has many older homes. Pat's construction background helps assess condition and renovation cost.

🔍 Sub-Area Differences

Prices, noise, schools and walkability vary a lot between sub-areas. Compare them individually.

How big is the Penticton market?
