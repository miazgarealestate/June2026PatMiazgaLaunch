Community Spotlight — Benchview Place, Osoyoos BC

A small 55+ building.
Nine units on 89th Street.

Benchview Place is a nine-unit strata building at 4801 89th Street in Osoyoos. MLS® listings show it was built in 1994 and describe it as a 55+ community with lake and mountain views.

Listings for Units 102 and 303 describe two-bedroom, two-bathroom homes of about 1,125–1,205 sq ft, two covered parking spaces (one a carport) and a storage locker. Unit details vary, so treat them as examples.

Who Benchview Place Is For

Buyers 55 and over who want a small, quiet building

With only nine units, Benchview Place is a small strata. Listings describe a well-maintained building with a strong sense of community, and homes with lake and mountain views, including a top-floor unit.

It is a 1994 building, so ask what has been replaced and what is coming. Roofs, windows, plumbing and electrical can be near replacement age, and in a nine-unit strata a single large repair is shared among few owners. The depreciation report and reserve fund show how that is planned for.

A buyer under 55 can own in a 55+ strata but may not live there unless an exemption applies. Read the bylaw before you decide.

What Buyers Need to Know

Buying at Benchview Place — the important details

🔞 The 55+ Bylaw

Listings call Benchview Place a 55+ community. In BC a strata can only restrict residents to age 55 and older. Read the bylaw for who may live in the unit, who is exempt, and how the strata applies it.

📄 New BC Depreciation Report Rule

As of 2024, deferring a depreciation report by vote is no longer allowed — South Okanagan strata corporations have until July 1, 2027 to be compliant if there is no report or the latest predates December 31, 2020. See the full guide →

🏠 A 1994 Building

Ask what has been replaced, when, and what is planned: roof, windows, siding, plumbing, electrical and the exterior stairs and balconies. Pat's construction background helps here.

💰 A Nine-Unit Strata

With few owners, one large repair is split few ways, and a single special levy can be significant. Ask for the reserve fund balance and the last three years of minutes.

🐾 Pets & Rentals

Listings say long-term rentals are allowed, but their pet wording differs. Get the current bylaws for pets and for rentals, which can change.

Frequently Asked Questions

Benchview Place — quick answers

How much do units at Benchview Place cost?

Benchview Place units sold between $285,000 and $352,500 over the past 24 months (2 sales, MLS® data to October 5, 2026). Currently active: $449,000.

Is Benchview Place a 55+ complex?

How many units are in Benchview Place?

When was Benchview Place built?

Are pets and rentals allowed at Benchview Place?

MLS® listings say long-term rentals are allowed. Pet wording differs between listings: one says one small pet is welcome and another lists pets as not allowed in its features. Get the current bylaws from the strata.

Can someone under 55 buy a unit at Benchview Place?

In BC an age-restriction bylaw applies to who lives in the unit, not to who owns it, so a buyer under 55 can own a unit in a 55+ strata but may not live there unless an exemption applies. The province requires exemptions for caregivers, spouses, children and adult children of qualifying residents, and residents who lived in a unit before the bylaw was passed are exempt.
