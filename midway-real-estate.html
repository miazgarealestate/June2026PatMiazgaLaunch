Community Spotlight — Midway, BC

A Kettle River village
priced below the Okanagan.

Midway is a small village in BC's Boundary Country, close to the Kettle River, with listings describing an Okanagan-like climate at lower prices.

Midway is in the Kootenay Boundary Regional District. The October 9, 2026 MLS® export shows 15 active listings, mostly building lots, a few homes and acreage, and one farm. Listings describe Midway as having the same great climate as the Okanagan without the high prices; that is a listing claim, not a statistic.

Why Midway

For buyers who want to build or want a quiet village

Midway suits buyers who want to build on a lot, install a modular home, or buy a quiet home in a small village.

Listings highlight lots of 73 x 110 ft for factory-built homes, a rancher on a third of an acre, and a log home with horse facilities. Details vary by property.

As in the rest of Boundary Country, verify services, wildfire exposure and winter access.

What Buyers Need to Know

Buying in Midway — the important details

🔌 Services

Confirm water, sewer, power and gas for any lot, and what it will cost to connect.

🏗️ Building or Modular

Factory-built homes need site work, foundations and permits. Pat's construction background helps estimate real costs.

🔥 Wildfire & Insurance

Ask your insurer early about coverage and premiums.

📜 Zoning

Confirm zoning and permitted uses with the Village of Midway or relevant authority.

🏞️ Rural Properties

Farm and acreage properties need water-rights, access and agricultural-use checks.

🛣️ Access & Winter

Check road maintenance and distance to services.

Frequently Asked Questions

Midway — quick answers

Is Midway in the Okanagan?

No. Midway is in Boundary Country along Highway 3, in the Kootenay Boundary Regional District.

Can I build a modular home in Midway?

Listings describe lots as suited to factory-built homes. Confirm zoning and building rules for the specific lot.

How much inventory is there?

The Oct 9, 2026 MLS® export shows 15 active listings, so choice is limited.
