Community Spotlight — Brightwater, Osoyoos BC

New build.
Concrete, not stick-frame.

Osoyoos' newest condo development, steps from the lake — four-storey concrete construction with an elevator, triple-paned windows, and a Phase II now selling from the mid $500,000s.

Brightwater is a genuine new-construction condo development on Lakeshore Drive, built by Span West and sold through Chamberlain Property Group. Phase II is a four-storey building with 22 suites, eight-inch concrete floors, dual-wall soundproofing, and a grade-level parkade — a notably different construction standard than the wood-frame buildings common in older Osoyoos strata communities.

Who Brightwater Is For

Buyers who want a genuinely new building, not a resale strata

Brightwater stands out in the Osoyoos market simply because it's new. Where most lakefront and near-lake strata options in town are older resort or condo-hotel conversions, Brightwater is purpose-built new construction — concrete floors and structure rather than wood frame, an elevator, secured heated parking, and modern insulation designed to keep utility costs down through both summer heat and winter cold.

All units are two-bedroom, two-bathroom in the open-concept layout marketed for Phase I, with Phase II adding a range up to 1,842 sq ft and three bedrooms in some units. Developer marketing for the building has emphasized no age, rental, or pet restrictions — a meaningful point of difference from many of the area's other resort-style communities, though as with any pre-construction or newly built purchase, buyers should confirm the actual registered bylaws once the strata is established, since developer marketing language and final bylaws can differ.

Being new construction, GST applies to the purchase price — a real cost that resale buyers in older buildings don't face, and one that should be built into any price comparison against comparable resale units.

What Buyers Need to Know

Buying at Brightwater — the important details

📄 New BC Depreciation Report Rule

As of 2024, deferring a depreciation report by vote is no longer allowed — South Okanagan strata corporations (including bare land strata) have until July 1, 2027 to be compliant. See the full guide →

📄 Disclosure Statement

Review the developer's disclosure statement in full — projected strata fees, warranty coverage, and completion timeline for any remaining units or phases.

💰 GST & New Home Programs

Confirm whether the BC Newly Built Home PTT exemption applies (full exemption up to $1.1M, partial to $1.15M) and how GST is calculated on the specific unit price.

🛡️ New Home Warranty

Confirm the building carries standard BC new home warranty coverage (2-5-10 year structure) and what's covered under each phase.

📜 Bylaws vs. Marketing

Developer marketing describing no age, rental or pet restrictions is not the same as the final registered strata bylaws. Get the actual bylaws once the strata corporation is established.
