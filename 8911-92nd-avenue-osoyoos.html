The Building

8911 92nd Avenue, Osoyoos

8911 92nd Avenue is a two-storey stucco building with a red tile roof, balconies on the upper level and covered carport parking, set back from the street behind landscaped beds and a brick address sign.

The units that have come up for sale are ground-floor, two-bedroom, two-bathroom condos. Listings have described vineyard and mountain views and a location close to shops, restaurants, wineries and walking trails. One listing put the beach about a six-minute walk away.

What Buyers Need to Know

Buying at 8911 92nd Avenue — questions to ask about the strata

📄 Strata Documents

Get the bylaws, minutes, financial statements, depreciation report or waiver, and any planned special levies before you write an offer.

🏷️ Strata Plan & Unit Count

Confirm the strata plan number, the number of units and who manages the building from the Form B or strata documents, rather than from listing remarks.

💰 Monthly Fees

Ask what the fee covers (insurance, water, grounds, contingency reserve) and how it has changed. Fees quoted in listings are for specific units at specific dates.

🐾 Pets & Rentals

Pet limits, rental and short-term rental rules and any age rules are set by the strata bylaws. Check the current bylaws, since listings may be out of date.

🏗️ Building Age & Condition

Ask about the roof, balconies and railings, stucco and envelope, plumbing and any completed or planned repairs. Listings give different build years, so confirm the year from title or strata records.
