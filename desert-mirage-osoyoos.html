The Community

What Desert Mirage actually is — and who buys here

Desert Mirage is a gated strata complex at 15 Solana Key Court in central Osoyoos, a short walk from Solana Bay on Osoyoos Lake, the beach and the downtown core. The complex sign reads 3204–3212 69th Avenue, so you will see both addresses. Per MLS® listings it has a Santa Fe design with archways and desert-toned exterior colours, an elevator, two outdoor pools and a hot tub. BC Assessment shows it as a four-storey wood-frame strata building, built 2003.

Units are the main story. BC Assessment shows two- and three-bedroom units of roughly 1,280–1,590 sq ft, and listings describe many as two bedrooms plus a den with two bathrooms, gas fireplaces and in-suite laundry. That is a lot of space for a condo, and it is why this complex suits full-time residents and second-home owners as well as investors.

⚠️ Rental rules — verify before buying for rental income

Several MLS® listings state that the complex allows long-term rentals with a 30-day minimum, small pets, and no age restriction. A 30-day minimum means nightly or weekly vacation rental is not what these listings describe. Strata bylaws can change, and the Town of Osoyoos has an STR principal-residence requirement (STRAA) for short-term rentals within town limits.

Before buying for rental income, get the current bylaws from the strata and confirm the minimum stay, any rental restrictions, and pet rules. Pat's Investment Property Guide covers the Osoyoos rules in more detail.

What to Know Before Buying

Desert Mirage purchase considerations

📄 New BC Depreciation Report Rule (2027 Deadline)

As of 2024, deferring a depreciation report by vote is no longer allowed — South Okanagan strata corporations have until July 1, 2027 to be compliant. See the full guide →

📄 Strata Documents

Review depreciation report, strata financials, bylaws, and meeting minutes before any offer. Pay particular attention to the reserve fund — resort strata pools and amenities require ongoing capital expenditure.

💰 Strata Fees

Two pools and a hot tub cost more to maintain than a standard strata. One MLS®-derived listing showed $390.65/month; confirm the current fee for the specific unit and what it includes.

🏊 Amenity Maintenance

Pools, hot tubs, and resort landscaping require regular capital outlay. A depreciation report will tell you the state of the reserve fund and whether special levies are likely on the horizon.

🔥 Wildfire Insurance

Get insurance confirmed as a subject condition — especially if purchasing in summer months. Insurers won't bind new policies within ~50km of an active wildfire. The strata carries building insurance; your personal condo policy covers contents and improvements.

📍 Unit Selection

Location within the complex matters. Lake-facing and pool-adjacent units are marketed as premium positions. Ground-floor units offer easy access and parking right outside; compare views and floors on the same day.

🤝 Renting It Out

If you will rent the unit while living elsewhere, plan for a property manager for tenant handling and maintenance, and factor the cost into your numbers.
