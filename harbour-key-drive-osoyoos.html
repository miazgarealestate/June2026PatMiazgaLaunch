Neighbourhood Spotlight — Harbour Key Drive, Osoyoos BC

A quiet street.
A private channel.

Freehold waterfront homes on a quiet, no-through street along the channel of Osoyoos Lake — from vacant building lots under $900,000 to fully custom estates over $2 million.

Harbour Key Drive is a residential street of individually owned, freehold homes positioned along the channel of Osoyoos Lake, offering direct water access and private docks in a quieter setting than the busier open-lake frontage elsewhere in town. Like Bayview Crescent, there's no strata here — every property is a standalone freehold purchase.

Who Harbour Key Drive Is For

Buyers who want either a custom build site or a finished waterfront estate

Harbour Key Drive works for two different buyer types simultaneously: those who want to design and build their own custom waterfront home from a vacant lot, and those who want a finished, move-in-ready property with an established dock and mature landscaping already in place. Both options exist on this street, sometimes just doors apart.

The channel-front position gives calmer water than open-lake frontage — genuinely useful for anyone planning to dock a boat long-term rather than just for recreational use. The street's quiet, no-through-traffic character is a repeated selling point in listings, and it sits within easy reach of downtown Osoyoos, wineries, and golf.

Because pricing spans from under $900,000 vacant lots to estates over $2 million, this street rewards buyers who do their homework on what's actually being offered — lot size, existing structures, and dock condition all move the number significantly.

What Buyers Need to Know

Buying on Harbour Key Drive — the important details

🏗️ Building on a Vacant Lot

If considering a lot purchase, confirm servicing status (water, sewer, power at lot line), any Riparian Areas Regulation report requirements, and current Town of Osoyoos zoning and setback rules for waterfront construction.

💧 Dock & Foreshore Tenure

Confirm the legal status of any existing dock — foreshore lease or tenure documentation should transfer cleanly with the sale.

🏠 Finished Home Inspection

For existing homes, a full inspection matters as much here as anywhere — Pat's construction background helps assess renovation quality on older waterfront homes.

💰 Total Cost of Ownership

No strata fee, but budget realistically for dock maintenance, waterfront insurance, and any shoreline protection work over time.

📐 Lot Size & Setbacks

Waterfront setback requirements affect how much of a vacant lot is actually buildable — confirm this before assuming a lot's full size is usable.
