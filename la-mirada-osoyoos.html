What Buyers Need to Know

Buying in La Mirada — the important details

📜 Strata Documents

Ask for the bylaws, rules, minutes, financial statements, insurance details and any special levy history. Have them reviewed before you remove subjects.

🐾 Rental and Pet Rules

Listings do not state whether pets or rentals are restricted. Some units are advertised for rent, but what the bylaws allow is not published. Get the current bylaws in writing.

🏊 Pool, Hot Tub and Elevator

Shared amenities cost money to run and replace. Ask what the fee covers, the age of the pool, hot tub and elevator equipment, and whether a contingency reserve is set aside for major work.

🌊 Lakefront Considerations

Ask about lake-level history, flood and erosion considerations, how moorage is handled, and the insurance cost and coverage for a waterfront building. Check the wildfire and insurance picture too.

🏗️ Building Condition

A 2009 frame building has several systems worth asking about: the roof, balconies and decks, exterior envelope, underground parking structure and mechanical equipment. Ask what has been inspected and what is planned. Pat's construction background helps with this review.
