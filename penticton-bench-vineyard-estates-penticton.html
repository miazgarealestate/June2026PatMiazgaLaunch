What Buyers Need to Know

Buying in Penticton Bench & Naramata Road Estates — the important details

🍇 ALR & Farm Rules

Agricultural Land Reserve land carries use and subdivision rules. Confirm status, permitted uses and any secondary-dwelling rules.

💧 Irrigation & Water Licences

Ask what water supply and licence the property has, and whether it transfers.

🌱 Crops & Contracts

Ask about grape or fruit contracts, vine age, equipment included and the farm income the owner reports.

💰 Closing Costs

Property transfer tax and other closing costs rise with price. See the BC Tax Guide and plan for them.
