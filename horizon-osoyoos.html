Community Spotlight — Horizon, Osoyoos BC

Five units.
A lake view each.

Horizon is a small, boutique townhome development in central Osoyoos — just five 3-bedroom units, marketed with lake views and walking distance to downtown.

Built by Maskeen, a Surrey-based developer with over 35 years in Metro Vancouver home building, Horizon appears to be one of the company's first projects outside the Lower Mainland. At only five units, this is about as small-scale as new construction gets in Osoyoos — genuinely boutique rather than a large complex, with the intimacy that comes from that scale and the limited availability that comes with it too.

Who Horizon Is For

Buyers who want new construction at a small, walkable scale

Horizon suits buyers drawn to new construction but put off by larger, higher-density developments. At just five units, it functions more like a small collection of individual townhomes than a typical multi-building complex — less shared infrastructure, likely a simpler strata, and a more intimate feel than Osoyoos' bigger new-build projects.

The central, walking-distance-to-downtown positioning is a genuine differentiator — many of Osoyoos' newer developments sit further from Main Street. For buyers who want new construction without giving up walkability, that's a real trade-off worth weighing against outlying options.

Being a small boutique project from a developer new to the Okanagan, buyers should treat this with the same due diligence as any new construction purchase — arguably more, given the limited track record of Maskeen specifically in this market compared to established local builders.

What Buyers Need to Know

Buying at Horizon — the important details

📄 Disclosure Statement

Review the developer's disclosure statement directly — projected strata fees, warranty coverage, and completion status for any remaining units, especially given the limited public information available.

🏗️ Builder Track Record

Maskeen's primary track record is in Metro Vancouver. Ask about their experience with Okanagan-specific building considerations — climate, soil, and local trades — before assuming Lower Mainland standards translate directly.

💰 GST & New Home Programs

Confirm whether the BC Newly Built Home PTT exemption applies at the unit's price point, and how GST is calculated on the total purchase price.

🛡️ New Home Warranty

Confirm standard BC new home warranty coverage (2-5-10 year structure) applies and get the specifics in writing rather than relying on marketing copy.

📜 Small Strata Considerations

A 5-unit strata operates differently than a large complex — fewer owners sharing costs can mean higher individual special-levy exposure. Understand the reserve fund and contingency planning before buying.

🔍 Pat's Assessment

Pat's construction and project management background is directly useful here — assessing build quality and completion standards on a smaller, less-established project matters more, not less.
