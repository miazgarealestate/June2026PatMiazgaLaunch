Community Spotlight — Casa Del Mila Oro Resort, Osoyoos BC

Santa Fe style.
300 feet of private beach.

Fully furnished, individually owned townhouses and condos on 300 feet of exclusive beachfront — a Santa Fe-styled resort that's been quietly owned and run by private owners since 2006.

Casa Del Mila Oro Resort at 5401 Lakeshore Drive is a Santa Fe-styled beachfront development of two-bedroom condos and three-bedroom townhouses, each individually owned. Owners furnish and manage their own units — many run their own short-term rental operations directly — with shared access to 300 feet of private beach, a heated pool and hot tub.

Who Casa Del Mila Oro Is For

Buyers who want a proven, established beachfront rental

Casa Del Mila Oro has operated since 2006, giving it a longer track record than many newer Osoyoos resort developments — useful for buyers who want to see how a property has actually performed over time rather than relying on projections. The Santa Fe architectural style and family-friendly shoreline have made it a repeat destination for the same guests year after year, which tends to support more predictable rental demand for owners who choose to rent.

Unit types run two-bedroom, one-bath condos around 800 sq ft, and larger three-bedroom, 2.5-bath townhouses around 1,600 sq ft with private garages — a meaningful size and price range within the same small community.

As with Lakeshore Villas, ownership and management here tends to be individual rather than centralized — owners handle their own bookings and guest relationships, which gives more control but also means due diligence should focus on the specific unit and owner's track record rather than a single building-wide standard.

What Buyers Need to Know

Buying at Casa Del Mila Oro Resort — the important details

📄 Strata Documents

Review bylaws, minutes, depreciation report and reserve fund status — standard due diligence for any strata purchase in this style of community.

📈 Rental Track Record

Since owners self-manage, ask the seller for actual booking history and reviews on their specific unit rather than relying on the resort's overall reputation.

⛴️ Dock & Moorage

Boat moorage is available at extra cost on the resort's dock — confirm current pricing and availability if that matters to you.

🐾 Pet & Guest Policies

Policies can vary by unit since owners self-manage — confirm what's allowed for the specific unit you're considering, especially if you plan to bring pets or rent to guests with pets.

💰 Total Cost of Ownership

Strata fees, property tax, insurance, and furnishing/maintenance costs for a rental-ready unit.
