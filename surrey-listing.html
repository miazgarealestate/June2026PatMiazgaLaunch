About This Property

A big Guildford home with a rare upside angle

This is a spacious 9-bedroom, 7-bathroom basement-entry home in one of North Surrey's most established neighbourhoods. At 4,525 sq ft total — 2,145 sq ft on the main and 2,380 sq ft below — there's genuine size here, plus a layout that already functions as a large family home or multi-generational property.

The main portion of the house has been refreshed with fresh paint and shows well — vacant and easy to view. Inside you'll find hardwood, laminate and mixed flooring, maple kitchen cabinets, and a well-laid-out floor plan across both levels. Outside: covered decks, a fenced yard, a greenhouse, an established garden, and both an apple and a plum tree — a genuinely usable outdoor space on a 60' x 140' lot.

Parking is a strong point for a lot this size: 10 total spaces, a double garage, front and lane access, and room for an RV. Lane access alone is a meaningful advantage in Guildford.

Location is walking distance to Guildford Mall, transit, schools and recreation, with quick access to Highway 1 and the Port Mann Bridge — relevant whether you're commuting into Vancouver or heading out to the Fraser Valley.

The Bigger Picture

Future development potential

Beyond the home itself, this is a prime Guildford holding property. City plans call for 4–6 storey condo development in this area — the kind of land-use shift that makes a lot like this interesting to both owner-occupiers and investors thinking longer-term.

That's worth a real conversation before you decide how to approach an offer — whether you're buying this as a family home, a holding property, or a future assembly play. Pat's construction and project management background is useful here: he can walk you through what "development potential" actually means in practical terms, and what it doesn't.
