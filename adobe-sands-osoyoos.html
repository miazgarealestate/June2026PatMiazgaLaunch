Community Spotlight — Adobe Sands, Osoyoos BC

A 55+ community.
Just above Peanut Lake.

Adobe Sands is a strata apartment complex at 8006 Vedette Drive in Osoyoos. An MLS® listing for Unit 101A shows it was built in 1992 and describes it as a 55+ community just above Peanut Lake.

The same listing describes ground-floor units with no stairs and their own enclosed sunroom or patio, plus covered parking and an extra storage unit, and says small pets are allowed. Unit details vary, so treat Unit 101A as one example.

Who Adobe Sands Is For

Single-level living in a 55+ building

Adobe Sands suits buyers aged 55 and over who want easy, low-maintenance, single-level living close to downtown Osoyoos. The Unit 101A listing describes a ground-floor home with its own enclosed sunroom or patio, covered parking and a storage unit.

It is also a 1992 building, so ask what has been replaced and what is coming. Roofs, windows, plumbing and electrical can be near replacement age, and the strata's depreciation report and reserve fund show how that is planned for.

A buyer under 55 can own in a 55+ strata but may not live there unless an exemption applies. Read the bylaw before you decide.

What Buyers Need to Know

Buying at Adobe Sands — the important details

🔞 The 55+ Bylaw

The listing calls Adobe Sands a 55+ community. In BC a strata can only restrict residents to age 55 and older. Read the bylaw for who may live in the unit, who is exempt, and how the strata applies it.

📄 New BC Depreciation Report Rule

Since July 1, 2024, a strata corporation with five or more lots (bare land stratas included) can no longer defer a depreciation report by a ¾ vote. Outside Metro Vancouver, the Fraser Valley and the Capital Regional District, a strata with no report, or only one dated before December 31, 2020, has until July 1, 2027 to obtain one. How much do units at Adobe Sands cost?

Adobe Sands units sold between $295,000 and $317,500 over the past 24 months, with a median of $305,000 (3 sales, MLS® data to October 5, 2026). Currently active: $309,000.

Is Adobe Sands a 55+ complex?

Yes, according to an MLS® listing for Unit 101A, which describes Adobe Sands as a 55+ community. Confirm the exact bylaw wording with the strata before you buy.

Can someone under 55 buy a unit at Adobe Sands?

In BC an age-restriction bylaw applies to who lives in the unit, not to who owns it, so a buyer under 55 can own a unit in a 55+ strata but may not live there unless an exemption applies. The province requires exemptions for caregivers, spouses, children and adult children of qualifying residents, and residents who lived in a unit before the bylaw was passed are exempt.

Are pets allowed at Adobe Sands?

An MLS® listing for Unit 101A states that small pets are allowed. Confirm the size and number limits in the strata bylaws.
