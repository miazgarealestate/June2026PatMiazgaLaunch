The Building

8909 92nd Avenue, Osoyoos

8909 92nd Avenue is a three-storey stucco building with a pitched roof and balconies on every level, set behind a stone retaining wall and landscaped beds with mature trees. A paved walkway leads from the street to the main entrance, between two brick pillars, one carrying the address.

The one unit that has come up for sale in the recent MLS® data was a ground-floor corner condo. The listing described vineyard and mountain views and a location within walking distance of Main Street, shopping, the post office, doctors, coffee shops, restaurants, the community centre and the library. A nearby building on the same street is 8911 92nd Avenue.

What Buyers Need to Know

Buying at 8909 92nd Avenue — questions to ask about the strata

📄 Strata Documents

Get the bylaws, minutes, financial statements, depreciation report or waiver, and any planned special levies before you write an offer.

🏷️ Strata Plan & Unit Count

Confirm the strata plan number, the number of units and who manages the building from the Form B or strata documents, rather than from listing remarks.

💰 Monthly Fees

Ask what the fee covers (insurance, water, grounds, contingency reserve) and how it has changed. Always confirm the current fee in the strata documents.

🐾 Pets & Rentals

Pet limits, rental and short-term rental rules and any age rules are set by the strata bylaws. Check the current bylaws, since listings may be out of date.

🏗️ Building Age & Condition

Ask about the roof, balconies and railings, stucco and envelope, plumbing and any completed or planned repairs. Only one listing gives a build year, so confirm it from title or strata records.
