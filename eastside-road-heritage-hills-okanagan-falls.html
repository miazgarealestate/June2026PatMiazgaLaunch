What Buyers Need to Know

Buying in Eastside Road & Heritage Hills — the important details

🌊 Foreshore, Dock & Beach

Confirm exactly what is owned versus Crown foreshore, and whether any dock or moorage is authorized. Ask for the survey and any rights-of-way.

💧 Lake Levels & Flood

Ask about high-water history, setbacks and any flood-construction requirements that apply to the lot.

⛰️ Hillside Sites

Slope, retaining walls, drainage and access matter on hillside lots. Ask for geotechnical and engineering documents.

💰 Closing Costs

Property transfer tax and other closing costs rise with price. See the BC Tax Guide and plan for them.
