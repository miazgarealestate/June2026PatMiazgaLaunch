Across the street
from your own beach.

Bare land strata RV lots on Lakeshore Drive, directly across from a private sandy beach reserved exclusively for residents — with an optional rental pool for income when you're not using it.

Island View RV Resort is a well-established, all-season RV lot community less than five minutes from downtown Osoyoos. Lots are bare land strata — you own the land itself, not a leased pad — and the resort's rental-friendly policy means owners can choose to place their lot in a rental program for extra income. Who Island View RV Resort Is For

RV owners who want land, not just a seasonal site rental

Island View works for buyers who already own — or plan to own — a motorhome or large trailer and want a permanent, titled home base for it rather than booking seasonal campground sites year after year. Because lots are bare land strata, you genuinely own the land your RV sits on, with the legal protections of BC's Strata Property Act.

The resort's private sandy beach, reserved exclusively for residents, sits directly across Lakeshore Drive. Amenities include a boat dock, playground, laundry facilities and common washroom/shower area — practical infrastructure for RV living rather than a resort-hotel experience.

The rental-friendly policy is a genuine differentiator: owners aren't required to use their lot personally every season, and can place it in the resort's rental program to generate income when it would otherwise sit empty.

What Buyers Need to Know

Buying at Island View RV Resort — the important details

📄 Strata Documents

Bare land strata still requires full document review — bylaws, minutes, reserve fund status for shared infrastructure like roads, docks and the beach area.

🚐 RV Size & Age Restrictions

Confirm the maximum RV length permitted on a specific lot (recent listings have ranged 28–36 ft) and any age or condition requirements in the bylaws.

⏱️ Move Fast

Lots here sell quickly once listed, and turnover is limited. If you're serious, be pre-approved for financing and ready to close quickly.

💵 Rental Pool Terms

Get the actual current rental program terms in writing — participation rules, revenue split, and any seasonal restrictions.

💰 Total Cost of Ownership

Strata fees, property tax on the land, and any utility hookup costs specific to the lot.
