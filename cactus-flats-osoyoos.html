Community Spotlight — Cactus Flats, Osoyoos BC

Park the RV.
Live upstairs.

A townhome with your motorhome parked inside — Cactus Flats pairs a fully finished upper-floor residence with an enclosed, heated garage built to fit RVs up to 47.5 feet. Built for snowbirds. Bought mostly by snowbirds.

Completed in 2009–2010, Cactus Flats remains one of the few Okanagan developments purpose-built around this exact need: secure, heated, enclosed storage for a motorhome, boat, or classic car, with a genuinely livable townhome suite above it. The developer's own sales history confirms the buyer profile — almost entirely Albertans, Vancouver Island residents, and Lower Mainland/Fraser Valley snowbirds.

Who Cactus Flats Is For

RV owners who want to live with their toys, not store them elsewhere

Cactus Flats solves a specific, real problem: where do you put a 40-foot motorhome when you're not on the road, in a town where most homes don't have anywhere close to that kind of garage space? Each townhome pairs a warm, contemporary upper-floor suite — hardwood or tile floors, quartz counters, gas fireplace, full-size laundry — with an enormous heated, fully enclosed lower-floor garage built specifically to fit large RVs, boats, or classic cars, complete with a 50 amp circuit to keep batteries charged.

The development's own sales data is telling: buyers came almost entirely from Alberta, Vancouver Island, and the Vancouver/Fraser Valley area, with strong representation from snowbirds specifically. This is a purpose-built lifestyle product, not a general residential townhome — and it tends to attract buyers who already know exactly why they want it.

Being a few hundred feet from Osoyoos Lake adds to the appeal, but the real differentiator is the garage — this is one of very few developments in the entire Okanagan built around this specific need.
