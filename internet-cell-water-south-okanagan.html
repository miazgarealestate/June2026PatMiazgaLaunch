Internet, cell & water — what to check before you buy

Coverage and water source vary block by block, not just town by town. Here's what actually matters for due diligence.

Internet & cell coverage

Coverage varies significantly depending on whether a property is in town, on the benches, or up on rural acreage like Anarchist Mountain. This is a due-diligence item worth checking for the specific address, not the general area — two properties a few minutes apart can have very different options.

In town (Osoyoos, Oliver)

Standard cable and fibre internet providers serve most in-town addresses, along with reliable cell coverage from the major Canadian carriers. This is generally comparable to service in any small BC town.

Rural & acreage properties

Options thin out with distance from town and elevation. Satellite internet (Starlink and similar services) has become the standard solution for many rural South Okanagan properties where cable doesn't reach, and generally performs well. Cell coverage on rural roads can have dead zones depending on terrain — worth testing your own carrier's signal at a specific property before relying on it.

For buyers: If remote work or reliable connectivity is a dealbreaker, confirm actual internet and cell service at the specific address — not just "the area generally has coverage" — before removing subjects. Pat can help verify this as part of due diligence on any property he's showing.

Water quality & supply

Water source is one of the most important and most overlooked due-diligence items for South Okanagan buyers, and it depends entirely on whether a property is on a municipal system or a private well.

Municipal water (in-town)

Properties within Osoyoos and Oliver town limits are typically on municipal water systems, which are treated and regularly tested to provincial drinking water standards. This is the simpler, lower-maintenance option and generally not a point of concern for buyers.

Private wells (rural)

Many rural and acreage properties — including most of Anarchist Mountain — rely on private wells. Well water quality and quantity (flow rate) both need to be verified independently before purchase; a well that looked fine to the seller for years can still have issues worth knowing about in advance. This is due diligence that goes well beyond a standard home inspection.

Radon is a separate but related consideration — the South Okanagan has elevated radon levels in some areas, which is a different issue from water quality but often comes up in the same due-diligence conversation for rural and older properties.

Common Questions

Is internet reliable in the South Okanagan?

In town, yes — standard cable/fibre providers and major cell carriers serve Osoyoos and Oliver comparably to any small BC town. On rural acreages, options thin out with distance and elevation, and satellite internet (such as Starlink) has become the standard solution for many properties where cable doesn't reach. Always confirm service at the specific address before buying if connectivity is a priority.

Do Osoyoos and Oliver have municipal water?

Properties within Osoyoos and Oliver town limits are typically on municipal water systems, treated and tested to provincial drinking water standards. Rural and acreage properties, including most of Anarchist Mountain, generally rely on private wells instead.

What should I check before buying a property on a well?

Both water quality (testing for contaminants and potability) and flow rate (quantity) should be independently verified before purchase, beyond what a standard home inspection covers. A well that has served the current owner adequately for years can still have issues worth knowing about in advance.

Is cell coverage good on Anarchist Mountain and other rural areas?

It varies by specific location and carrier due to terrain. Coverage along main roads is generally reliable, but rural side roads can have dead zones. It's worth testing your own carrier's signal at a specific property before relying on it, especially if you work remotely or need consistent connectivity.
