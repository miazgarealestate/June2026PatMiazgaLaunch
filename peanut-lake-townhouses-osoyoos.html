Community Spotlight — Peanut Lake Townhouses, Osoyoos BC

Townhouses above the lake.
Found by address.

A row of attached strata townhouses on the odd-numbered side of Vedette Drive, 8005 to 8043, overlooking Peanut Lake in Osoyoos. There is no official complex name, so buyers search by street address.

MLS® listings from 2025 show a 1981 build, two- and three-bedroom layouts of roughly 1,490 to 1,630 sq ft over multiple floors, and the Peanut Lake setting. This is an older strata townhouse row, so the buying questions are about roofs, envelope, decks, slope and the strata's finances, not finishes.

Who These Townhouses Are For

Townhouse space near downtown, in an older build

These townhouses suit buyers who want a multi-floor attached home with lake outlook close to downtown Osoyoos, and who are comfortable with a 1981 building. Listings from 2025 describe two- and three-bedroom layouts with walkout levels and decks overlooking Peanut Lake.

The trade-off is age. Roofs, stucco, decks, windows and drainage on a sloping lakeside site matter more here than in a newer complex, and the strata's reserve fund and depreciation report show how those costs are being handled.

Also on Vedette Drive: The Villas, a newer townhome development at 8000, and Adobe Sands, a 55+ complex at 8006.

What Buyers Need to Know

Buying a Peanut Lake townhouse — the important details

📄 New BC Depreciation Report Rule

Since July 1, 2024, a strata corporation with five or more lots (bare land stratas included) can no longer defer a depreciation report by a ¾ vote. Outside Metro Vancouver, the Fraser Valley and the Capital Regional District, a strata with no report, or only one dated before December 31, 2020, has until July 1, 2027 to obtain one. See the full guide →

📜 Strata Documents

Get the bylaws, the last two to three years of minutes, financial statements and the reserve fund balance. Confirm the strata corporation and plan number for the exact address.

🏠 1981 Build: Roof & Envelope

Ask when roofs, stucco, windows and decks were last repaired or replaced, and whether envelope inspections have been done. Many units overlook the lake and have decks.

⛰️ Slope & Drainage

The row sits above the lake on sloping ground. Ask about drainage, retaining structures and any past water problems or movement.

🐾 Rental, Pet & STR Rules

Set by each strata's bylaws and can change. Osoyoos also opted into the provincial short-term rental principal-residence requirement on November 1, 2024. Confirm both for the unit.

🔍 Pat's Assessment

Pat's construction background is useful for reading roofs, envelope, decks and drainage on an older lakeside townhouse. Ask for recent sold comparables on Vedette Drive.

Frequently Asked Questions

Peanut Lake townhouses — quick answers

How much do the Peanut Lake townhouses cost?

No sale at 8005–8043 Vedette Drive (odd numbers) appears in the MLS® data for the past 24 months. One townhome at 8029 Vedette Drive is listed at $454,900 (2 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms, built 1981). Ask Pat for current comparables.

Which addresses are part of the Peanut Lake townhouses?

The 20 odd-numbered addresses on Vedette Drive, Osoyoos: 8005, 8007, 8009, 8011, 8013, 8015, 8017, 8019, 8021, 8023, 8025, 8027, 8029, 8031, 8033, 8035, 8037, 8039, 8041, 8043.

Do the Peanut Lake townhouses have an official complex name?

No official complex name has been found. The townhouses are identified by street address, and MLS® listings use Peanut Lake as the community name.

Can I rent out a townhouse here as a short-term rental?

Osoyoos opted into the province's short-term rental principal-residence requirement on November 1, 2024. The strata's own bylaws also apply, so check both before buying.
