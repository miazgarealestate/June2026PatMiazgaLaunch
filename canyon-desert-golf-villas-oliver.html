Who Canyon Desert Golf Villas Is For

Buyers who want a low-maintenance villa on the golf course and understand a lease

Canyon Desert Golf Villas suits buyers who want a one-level home with a garage in a gated setting beside the golf course, and who are comfortable owning a home on leased land.

That is the key point. At Sunningdale Estates, also on Tucelnuit Drive, MLS® listings describe a bare land strata where owners hold title to their lots. Here, per MLS® listings, title is leasehold, so you should know the lessor, the remaining term and what happens at the end of the lease before you make an offer.

What Buyers Need to Know

Buying at Canyon Desert Golf Villas — the important details

📜 Leasehold, Not Freehold

MLS® listings show leasehold title and mention a pre-paid lease on Crown land. You would own the home, not the land, so the lease terms matter as much as the home itself.

🔑 Who the Lessor Is and How Long the Lease Runs

Listings do not agree on enough detail to rely on. Confirm who holds the head lease, the remaining term, whether it can be renewed and at what cost, and what consent or fees apply when the lease is assigned to a new owner. Have your lawyer review it before you write an offer.

📄 Strata Documents

Review the bylaws, minutes, financial statements, depreciation report, contingency reserve and any planned special levies. Ask what the monthly fee includes. See the depreciation report guide →

👥 Adult Community Rule

The sign says “An Adult Community”, but listing wording about age restrictions is not consistent. Get the current registered bylaw, including how it applies to a spouse, partner or future owner.

🐾 Pets and Rentals

Listing remarks mention both, but may describe one unit. Read the current bylaws and rules, and ask about any limits on number, size or rental terms.

⛳ Golf Course Setting

Homes back onto or sit near the fairway. Ask whether golf or club access is included or separate, and what the setting means for noise, errant golf balls and views.

🏗️ Roofs, Stucco and Mechanicals

Homes appear to date from the early 2010s, so the stucco, roof and mechanicals are not new but not old. Ask about the wall envelope, roof, furnace and air conditioning, and whether any common elements are due for work.

Frequently Asked Questions

Canyon Desert Golf Villas — quick answers

Is Canyon Desert Golf Villas an adult community?

The entrance sign reads “An Adult Community”. Listings word the age rule in different ways, so confirm the exact rule, including how it applies to spouses, partners and future buyers, in the registered bylaws before you write an offer.

Where is Canyon Desert Golf Villas?

Canyon Desert Golf Villas is at 6823 Tucelnuit Drive, Oliver, BC. MLS® listings place it on the Nk’Mip Canyon Desert Golf Course, and Pat’s photos show the fairway and hillside beside the community.

Do you own the land at Canyon Desert Golf Villas?

MLS® listings show leasehold title and mention a pre-paid lease on Crown land. You would own the home but not the land, so confirm who holds the head lease, how long the lease runs, whether it can be renewed and what consents or fees apply when you buy and sell.

Is Canyon Desert Golf Villas gated?

Pat’s photos show a black metal gate and fencing at the entrance, and listings describe a gated, golf cart-friendly community. Ask how access works for visitors and residents.

What are the strata fees at Canyon Desert Golf Villas?

MLS® listings describe a low monthly fee, in a range of roughly $150 to $250. Fees can differ by unit and change over time, so confirm the current amount, what it covers and what the financial statements show.

Are pets and rentals allowed at Canyon Desert Golf Villas?

Listing remarks mention pets and rentals, but the wording is not consistent and may describe one unit. Read the current registered bylaws and rules rather than listing remarks.

What kind of homes are at Canyon Desert Golf Villas?

Pat’s photos show one-level stucco homes with tile-style roofs, attached garages and low-maintenance gravel landscaping. Listings describe villas and half-duplex ranchers built in the early 2010s. Details vary by home.
