About Sunshine Cove

A small lakefront complex with its own beach

Two MLS® listings for units at 4231 Oleander Drive describe Sunshine Cove as “one of the fewest waterfront complexes in sunny Osoyoos.” They mention beautiful landscaping, moorage, a dock, an indoor pool, a sauna and a whirlpool, and say the complex is “minutes to downtown, golf and all amenities.”

The units in those listings are level-entry townhomes of roughly 1,300 sq ft with two bedrooms and two bathrooms. Both face east toward the lake and mountains. On-site photos show single-storey buildings with covered carports, rock-and-grass landscaping and mountain views.

Sunshine Cove is Strata Plan KAS466 and has 35 units, per BC Assessment. The listings do not say whether any age or rental restrictions apply or how moorage is allocated, so ask for those details early.

What Buyers Need to Know

Buying in Sunshine Cove — the important details

📜 Strata Documents

Ask for the bylaws, rules, minutes, financial statements, insurance details and any special levy history. Have them reviewed before you remove subjects.

⛵ Moorage and Dock

Ask how moorage is assigned, whether it comes with the unit or is separate, what boats it suits, and what it costs. Confirm the rules on lake-access structures and any dock repairs planned.

🏊 Pool, Sauna and Whirlpool

Shared amenities cost money to run and replace. Ask what the fee covers, the age of the equipment, and whether a contingency reserve is set aside for major work.

🌊 Lakefront Considerations

Ask about lake-level history, flood and erosion considerations, and the insurance cost and coverage for a waterfront building. Check the wildfire and insurance picture too.

🏗️ 1980s Construction

Ask about the age and condition of the roof, windows, plumbing, wiring and heating and cooling. Listings describe the examples as needing some updating. Pat's construction background helps with renovation costs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Sunshine Cove — quick answers

Where is Sunshine Cove in Osoyoos?

Sunshine Cove is at 4231 Oleander Drive on the west shore of Osoyoos Lake in Osoyoos, BC. MLS® listings describe about 700 feet of private sandy beach and say it is minutes to downtown and golf.

What amenities does Sunshine Cove have?

MLS® listings mention a private sandy beach, moorage, a dock, an indoor pool, a sauna, a whirlpool and landscaped grounds. Ask for the strata documents to confirm what is shared and what it costs.

How much do Sunshine Cove townhomes cost?

Unit 31 sold for $550,000 in August 2025 and unit 27 is listed at $449,900 (MLS® data to October 5, 2026). With one sale and one listing, treat this as a snapshot, not a trend.

What should I check before buying in Sunshine Cove?

Request the bylaws, minutes, financial statements, insurance details and fee, then ask how moorage is assigned and what the pool, dock and beach cost to maintain. Listings do not state the fee or any age or rental rules.
