Community Spotlight — Regal Ridge, Anarchist Mountain

Wooded estate lots.
A quieter corner of the mountain.

Regal Ridge — also known locally as Osoyoos Mountain Estates — is a wooded estate-lot subdivision on Anarchist Mountain, developed in the early 2000s within RDOS Electoral Area A above Osoyoos.

Pat lives on Anarchist Mountain himself, and Regal Ridge is one of its named pockets — a subdivision of wooded estate lots that's gone by two names over the years, appearing on some records as Regal Ridge and on others as Osoyoos Mountain Estates. Whichever name comes up in your search, it's the same community: larger lots, tree cover, and the same panoramic valley and lake views that define Anarchist Mountain living, a short drive up the switchbacks from Osoyoos.

Why Regal Ridge

Anarchist Mountain living, in a named estate-lot pocket

Regal Ridge suits the same buyer Anarchist Mountain generally attracts — someone drawn to elevation, tree cover, panoramic valley and lake views, and genuine distance from town, without giving up a manageable commute into Osoyoos. As a named subdivision within the broader mountain, it offers a slightly more defined community feel than a scattered rural address.

Being wooded, estate-sized lots developed as a purpose-built subdivision in the early 2000s, properties here tend to share a certain scale and character — larger parcels than a typical in-town lot, with the tree cover and privacy that draws people to Anarchist Mountain in the first place.

Because it carries two names in different records, it's worth confirming which one a specific listing or legal document uses before assuming you're looking at the same or a different property.

🎯 Honest assessment — who Regal Ridge is right for

If you need to be walkable to downtown Osoyoos or want lake-adjacent living, Anarchist Mountain generally — and Regal Ridge specifically — isn't the fit; look at in-town Osoyoos communities instead.

Regal Ridge is the right choice for buyers who want: an established, named estate-lot community on Anarchist Mountain, wooded privacy with valley/lake views, and the mountain's rural character with a defined subdivision feel rather than scattered acreage.

Pat's own experience living on Anarchist Mountain is directly useful here — real day-to-day knowledge of commute times, winter driving, and what living up the switchbacks actually involves, not just listing data.

What to Know

Buying in Regal Ridge — the real considerations

📋 Name Confusion

Regal Ridge and Osoyoos Mountain Estates refer to the same subdivision. Confirm which name a title, listing, or bylaw document uses so you're comparing apples to apples.

🚗 Switchback Commute

Anarchist Mountain access is via Highway 3's switchbacks — manageable daily, but a real winter-driving consideration. Confirm your comfort level before committing to full-time residence.

🏘️ RDOS Area A Rules

Regal Ridge falls under RDOS Electoral Area A. There's no principal-residence requirement here like there is in Osoyoos town, but short-term rentals still require an RDOS STR Permit ($500 fee) and, in most cases, a business licence — waived only when the operator lives on-site with guests. Zoning permits up to two STR uses per property. Verify current regulations directly with RDOS Development Services.

💧 Well Water & Septic

Like most of Anarchist Mountain, properties here typically run on well water and septic. Both should be properly inspected as part of any purchase.

🔥 Wildfire Considerations

Wooded mountain properties carry wildfire risk and insurance considerations that in-town properties don't. Understand FireSmart standards and current insurance availability before purchasing.

✅ Speculation Tax

Anarchist Mountain sits outside the SVT designated taxable area — it simply isn't declared. Always confirm current designations before purchasing.
