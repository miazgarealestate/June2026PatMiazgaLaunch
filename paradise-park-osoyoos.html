How the Ownership Works

Ownership in a community like this can be a pad lease, a bare land strata, a share in the land or another structure. The fee alone does not tell you which.

Ownership: what to confirm

Ask for the title search, the park or strata agreement, the rules and a written list of what the monthly or annual fee covers. Whether you own land, a share or only your home changes your financing, your resale position and your protection if the property is sold or redeveloped.

Pad rental, strata or share: why it matters

In a standard manufactured home park, you own your home but rent the land beneath it. The park owner can raise pad rent (for 2026 the limit is 2.3% plus a proportional amount for changes in local-government levies and regulated utility fees, and a landlord can apply for an additional increase), and if the park ever sells or redevelops, tenants have limited protection. Other structures, such as a bare land strata or a share in the land, work differently. That is why you should confirm which one applies here before you make an offer.

Location & Neighbourhood

5901 Main Street is near downtown Osoyoos, within walking distance of coffee, groceries and restaurants. Confirm what lake access, if any, comes with a home here.

The surrounding neighbourhood is a mix of residential homes, resorts, and established parks. It's a quiet pocket despite being central — Main Street traffic is low once you turn off the highway strip.
