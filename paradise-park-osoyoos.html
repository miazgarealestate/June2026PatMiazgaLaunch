How the Ownership Works

Paradise Park is fundamentally different from a typical manufactured home park where you pay monthly pad rent to a landlord who owns the land. Here, each home owner holds a proportional share of the entire 3.45-acre freehold property — the land belongs collectively to the owners, not to a third party.

Freehold Park Share — What That Means in Plain Terms

When you buy a home in Paradise Park, your purchase includes a fractional ownership interest in the whole property — the waterfront, the pool, the dock, the land under your home, all of it. There is no landlord to increase your pad rent. The owners collectively manage the shared property through a strata-like agreement. This is a far more secure form of tenure than a standard leasehold pad rental, and generally easier to finance.

Freehold vs. Pad Rental — Why It Matters

In a standard manufactured home park, you own your home but rent the land beneath it. The park owner can raise pad rent (for 2026 the limit is 2.3% plus a proportional amount for changes in local-government levies and regulated utility fees, and a landlord can apply for an additional increase), and if the park ever sells or redevelops, tenants have limited protection. In a freehold park like Paradise Park, the residents own the land collectively. There's no outside landlord, no pad rent increases at someone else's discretion, and the waterfront is yours. It's a fundamentally stronger ownership position — and one of the reasons properties here hold their value better than comparable leasehold parks.

Location & Neighbourhood

5901 Main Street sits on the eastern shore of Osoyoos Lake, just a short walk from the heart of downtown Osoyoos. You can walk to coffee, groceries, restaurants, and the public beach areas. The lake here is warm — Osoyoos Lake is Canada's warmest freshwater lake — and the park's private beach and dock give you the kind of access that most waterfront buyers spend considerably more to find.

The surrounding neighbourhood is a mix of residential homes, resorts, and established parks. It's a quiet pocket despite being central — Main Street traffic is low once you turn off the highway strip.
