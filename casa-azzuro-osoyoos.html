About Casa Azzuro

A very small waterfront strata, built in 2006

The MLS® listing for unit 1 calls Casa Azzuro a four-unit waterfront complex and a “family orientated small complex.” It describes lake views from the primary bedroom and the kitchen and dining area, a gas fireplace, central air conditioning, two covered decks with gas barbecue hookups, a double garage, and a storage room or workshop with a separate entrance. It also mentions a boat slip.

BC Assessment lists four strata townhouses at 5405 Oleander Drive, numbered 1 to 4 and recorded as three-storey units with garages built in 2006. Their strata areas are between 1,403 and 1,423 sq ft, and all four are two-bedroom. The unit 1 listing states 1,563 sq ft, so the two sources differ on size. Confirm measurements before relying on either.

The listing places it within walking distance of downtown, the community centre, the library and shops. Listings do not say what the strata fee is, whether any age, pet or rental restrictions apply, or what the boat slip includes. Ask for those details early.

What Buyers Need to Know

Buying in Casa Azzuro — the important details

📜 Strata Documents

Ask for the bylaws, rules, minutes, financial statements, insurance details and any special levy history. Have them reviewed before you remove subjects.

🤝 A Four-Unit Strata

With only four owners, one large repair is shared four ways and decisions depend on very few votes. Ask how the contingency reserve is funded and how repairs and insurance are handled.

⛵ Boat Slip

The unit 1 listing mentions a boat slip. Ask whether it is assigned to each unit, how it is documented, what boats it suits, what it costs, and whether it transfers when you sell.

🌊 Lakefront Considerations

Ask about lake-level history, flood and erosion considerations, and the insurance cost and coverage for a waterfront building. Check the wildfire and insurance picture too.

🏗️ 2006 Construction

Ask about the roof, decks, stucco and exterior envelope, windows, and mechanical equipment, and what has been inspected or planned. Three storeys means stairs, which matters if you are downsizing. Pat's construction background helps with this review.
