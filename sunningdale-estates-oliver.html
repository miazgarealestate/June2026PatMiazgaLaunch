Who Sunningdale Estates Is For

Buyers 55+ who want a one-level rancher and a lot of their own

Sunningdale Estates suits buyers aged 55 and over who want one-level living with an attached garage in a managed community, and who prefer to hold title to their lot rather than lease a pad.

That last point matters on Tucelnuit Drive. Nearby communities such as Tradewinds Estates are manufactured home parks where the home sits on a rented pad. A bare land strata is a different ownership structure, with different costs, rules and resale considerations.

MLS® listings place it close to the golf course, Tuc-el-Nuit Lake, the hospital, the arena and town, and describe it as secure and low maintenance.

What Buyers Need to Know

Buying at Sunningdale Estates — the important details

📜 What “Bare Land Strata” Means

The lot owner holds title to the lot and the home on it, and the strata corporation manages the common property. Ask exactly what the strata owns and maintains here, such as roads, the entrance walls and hedging and any shared fencing, and what you maintain yourself.

📄 Strata Documents

Review the bylaws, minutes, financial statements, depreciation report, contingency reserve and any planned special levies before you write an offer.

👥 55+ Rules

BC’s Strata Property Act allows an age-restriction bylaw, set at no less than 55. Confirm how this strata applies it, including to spouses, partners and future owners.

🐾 Pets, Rentals & RV Parking

The current listing allows two small pets and mentions limited RV parking. Earlier listings worded the pet rule differently, and listing remarks conflict on rentals. Rely on the registered bylaws, not listing remarks.

💵 Reserve Fund

A low monthly fee can mean a small contingency reserve. Compare the depreciation report with what the fee actually covers.

🏗️ Home Age & Mechanicals

These are 1990s-era ranchers, and year-built details in listings vary by unit. Ask about the roof, furnace or heat pump, hot water tank, windows and crawl space, and whether any original polybutylene (Poly-B) plumbing remains. The current Unit 40 listing mentions new plumbing; confirm per home.

💰 Total Cost of Ownership

Strata fee, property tax, home insurance, any RV parking charge and any levies. Confirm exactly what the monthly fee covers.

📄 BC Depreciation Report Rule

Since July 1, 2024, a strata corporation with five or more lots (bare land stratas included) can no longer defer a depreciation report by a ¾ vote. Outside Metro Vancouver, the Fraser Valley and the Capital Regional District, a strata with no report, or only one dated before December 31, 2020, has until July 1, 2027 to obtain one. Frequently Asked Questions

Sunningdale Estates — quick answers

Is Sunningdale Estates a 55+ community?

MLS® listings describe Sunningdale Estates as a 55+ adult community. BC's Strata Property Act allows a strata to adopt an age-restriction bylaw, but not one set below 55, so the exact rule is whatever is in the registered bylaws. Confirm how it applies to spouses, partners and future owners before you write an offer.

Where is Sunningdale Estates?

Sunningdale Estates is at 6526 Tucelnuit Drive, Oliver, BC V0H 1T3. MLS® listings describe it as close to the golf course, Tuc-el-Nuit Lake, the hospital, the curling club, the arena, Oliver's community park and the riverfront hike and bike trail.

Do you own the land at Sunningdale Estates?

MLS® listings describe Sunningdale Estates as a bare land strata. In a bare land strata the owner holds title to their own lot and home, and the strata corporation looks after the common property. Confirm exactly what is on your title and what the strata maintains before you buy.

Are pets allowed at Sunningdale Estates?

The current MLS® listing says two small pets are allowed. Earlier listings worded it differently, for example one dog, one cat or both, to a maximum of two. Pet bylaws can change, so check the current registered bylaws.

Is there RV parking at Sunningdale Estates?

MLS® listings, including the current one, mention limited RV parking. Ask the strata how spaces are assigned, what they cost and whether there is a waiting list.

What are the strata fees at Sunningdale Estates?

The current MLS® listing (Unit 40, October 2026) states strata fees of $45 per month. Fees can change and may differ by unit, so confirm the current amount and what it covers with the strata, and ask for the financial statements.

Can you rent out a home at Sunningdale Estates?

Listing remarks conflict on this. One earlier listing said no rentals are permitted, while another said rentals are allowed with restrictions. Do not rely on listing remarks; read the current registered bylaws and any rental rules before you decide.
