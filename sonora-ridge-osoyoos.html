Community Spotlight — Sonora Ridge, Osoyoos BC

Gated, custom-built,
one block from the beach.

An exclusive gated community of Southwest-inspired custom homes on Osoyoos's East Bench at 4110 36th Avenue — one block from the lake, five minutes to downtown, with sweeping views over the valley and Osoyoos Lake.

Sonora Ridge is a well-established gated strata community where custom-built homes sit on view lots along the East Bench, most walking distance to the beach. Recent sales have ranged from $950,000 to just over $1,060,000, and a handful of building lots remain for buyers who want to bring their own architect. New builds here have leaned into open-concept layouts, chef's kitchens, and finishes like built-in elevators and dual gas fireplaces — this is a step above the typical resort-strata product elsewhere in Osoyoos.

Who Sonora Ridge Is For

Buyers who want a custom home in a gated setting, near the beach

Sonora Ridge sits on the East Bench above Osoyoos, a short walk to the lake and about five minutes to downtown. It's a step up from the resort-strata product found elsewhere in town — homes here are individually designed, several with Southwest/Santa Fe architectural influences, and recent sales have shown finishes like built-in elevators, chef's kitchens, and dual gas fireplaces.

With sale prices in the high $900,000s to low $1,000,000s and remaining lots priced around $299,900, this suits buyers building or buying at the top end of the Osoyoos market rather than those looking for an entry-level or investment-focused purchase. Views over the valley, Osoyoos Lake, and the South Okanagan hillsides are a consistent selling point across listings here.

Because this is a gated strata community, due diligence should include a review of the strata's bylaws, fees, and reserve fund — the specifics haven't been confirmed yet for this page and should be verified for any unit you're considering.
