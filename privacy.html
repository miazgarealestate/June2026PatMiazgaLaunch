Privacy Policy

How personal information submitted through patmiazga.com is collected and used.

Who we are

This website is operated by Pat Miazga, licensed REALTOR® with RE/MAX All Points Realty (BCFSA Lic. #138735), serving the South Okanagan and related BC markets.

What we collect

When you use the contact form on this site, Pat may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Interest / inquiry type
  • Message content

The forms that unlock the full version of a guide collect similar details: your name, email, phone number (optional), and one or two questions about your situation, such as where you are moving from or your timeline.

Why we collect it

Pat uses this information to respond to your inquiry and to provide the real estate information and services you asked about — such as buying, selling, relocating, or investing in the South Okanagan and elsewhere in British Columbia. Pat may contact you by email or phone for that purpose.

Submitting a guide form unlocks the rest of that guide on the page. It does not send you a copy of the guide and does not sign you up for automatic emails or a newsletter.

How it is stored and accessed

Contact and guide form submissions are sent to Formspree (formspree.io), a third-party form service, which delivers them to Pat. Formspree handles submissions under its own privacy policy. Pat then handles your message through business email and phone.

If a submission cannot be sent, the form tells you so and the guide stays locked.

Your browser also stores small items on your own device: a setting that remembers you have unlocked a guide, and, after a successful guide submission, a copy of the details you entered. These stay in your browser, are not sent anywhere by this storage, and can be cleared in your browser settings.

This site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when pages load.

Pat does not sell personal information and does not use form submissions for unrelated third-party marketing lists.

Your choices

You may contact Pat to ask for an update or deletion of inquiry data held about you, subject to any records we must keep for legal, regulatory, or brokerage compliance reasons.
