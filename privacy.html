Who we are

This website is operated by Pat Miazga, licensed REALTOR® with RE/MAX All Points Realty (BCFSA Lic. #138735), serving the South Okanagan and related BC markets.

What we collect

When you use the contact form on this site, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Interest / inquiry type
  • Message content

Guide download / lead forms on related pages may collect similar contact details (for example name, email, phone, location, and timeline).

Why we collect it

We use this information to respond to your inquiry and to provide real estate information and services you requested — such as answering questions about buying, selling, relocating, or investing in the South Okanagan and elsewhere in British Columbia.

How it is stored and accessed

Contact form submissions are delivered to Pat’s form inbox via Formspree (formspree.io). Messages are then handled through Pat’s business email and phone workflows.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use form submissions for unrelated third-party marketing lists.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask for an update or deletion of inquiry data we hold about you, subject to any records we must keep for legal, regulatory, or brokerage compliance reasons.
