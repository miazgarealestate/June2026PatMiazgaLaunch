Who Cherry Grove Estates Is For

Buyers 55+ who want a lower entry price and a park with a clubhouse

Cherry Grove Estates suits buyers aged 55 and over who want a manufactured home in a managed community with shared amenities, and who are comfortable paying monthly pad rent in exchange for a lower purchase price than a home on its own land.

That trade-off is the key point. You typically own the home but rent the pad, so the park’s rules, rent increases and tenancy terms shape your costs and your resale. Nearby, Sunningdale Estates is a different structure, described in MLS® listings as a bare land strata where owners hold title to their lots.

MLS® listings place the park near the Nk’Mip Canyon Desert Golf Course, Tuc-el-Nuit Lake, the hospital and shopping, and one listing describes it as within walking distance of town.

What Buyers Need to Know

Buying at Cherry Grove Estates — the important details

🏠 Own the Home, Rent the Pad

In a manufactured home park you typically own the home and pay monthly pad rent for the lot under it. That is why purchase prices are lower than for a home on its own land, and why the park's rules, rent increases and tenancy terms matter as much as the home. Ask for the tenancy agreement and park rules before you write an offer.

📜 Confirm the Ownership Structure

Sources conflict on how Cherry Grove Estates is structured. A park directory describes a land lease with a private owner, while one current listing calls the monthly charge a strata maintenance fee. Ask for the title search and the documents that apply to your purchase, and have your lawyer explain what you would be buying.

💵 Pad Rent and What It Covers

Listings from 2024 to 2026 mention pad rent of roughly $450 to $550 a month, and several say it covers water, sewer, garbage and snow removal. Get the current rate, the increase history for the last few years and a written list of what is included.

👥 55+ and Park Rules

Listings describe the park as 55+, and one says all occupants must be 55 or older. Pets are allowed with restrictions, so confirm the number, size and type permitted, and listings that mention rentals say none are allowed. Confirm each rule in writing with the park, including how it applies to a spouse or partner under 55.

🔧 Home Age and Mechanicals

Per MLS® listings, homes in the park were built from the late 1970s to the early 1980s, so many have had updates. Ask about the roof, furnace or heat pump, hot water tank, windows, electrical and skirting, and whether any original plumbing remains. Some listings mention updated systems; confirm per home.

🪵 Additions, Sunrooms and Sheds

Many listings mention enclosed sunrooms, covered decks, workshops and sheds. Ask whether each addition was built with approvals and whether the park signed off, because unapproved additions can complicate resale and insurance.

🏦 Financing and Insurance

Lenders and insurers can be more cautious with older manufactured homes on rented pads. Get an insurance quote and talk to your lender or credit union before you remove subjects.

Frequently Asked Questions

Cherry Grove Estates — quick answers

Is Cherry Grove Estates a 55+ community?

MLS® listings consistently describe Cherry Grove Estates as a 55+ community, and one recent listing states that all occupants must be 55 or older. The exact rule, including how it applies to spouses, partners and future buyers, is set by the park's rules and your tenancy documents. Confirm it in writing before you write an offer.

Where is Cherry Grove Estates?

Cherry Grove Estates is at 6601 Tucelnuit Drive, Oliver, BC. MLS® listings describe it as beside the Nk'Mip Canyon Desert Golf Course and close to Tuc-el-Nuit Lake, the hospital and shopping.

Do you own the land at Cherry Grove Estates?

Sources conflict, so confirm this before you buy. A park directory describes Cherry Grove Estates as a land-lease community with a privately held owner, and MLS® listings refer to a monthly pad rent. One current listing, however, labels the monthly charge a strata maintenance fee and the title type leasehold. In a typical manufactured home park you own the home and rent the pad it sits on. Ask for the title search, the tenancy agreement and the park rules to see exactly what you would own and what you would pay for.

How much is pad rent at Cherry Grove Estates?

MLS® listings from 2024 to 2026 mention monthly pad rent in the range of roughly $450 to $550. Several listings say it covers water, sewer, garbage collection and snow removal, and some mention on-site management. Rent can change, so get the current amount, the increase history and a written list of what is included from the park.

Are pets allowed at Cherry Grove Estates?

Pets are allowed with restrictions. Listing wording varies, from one small indoor pet to pets welcome with restrictions, and some older listings said no pets. Ask the park for the current rule in writing, including the number, size and type of pet permitted, before you decide.

Can you rent out a home at Cherry Grove Estates?

MLS® listings that address rentals say they are not allowed. Confirm the current rule with the park, including what happens if you need to move out but want to keep the home.

What amenities does Cherry Grove Estates have?

Per MLS® listings, the park has a clubhouse with a hall and kitchen, billiards room, library, shuffleboard, an exercise room and a woodworking shop, and one listing mentions on-site management. Ask the park what is included in pad rent and whether any amenity has a separate fee.

What do homes at Cherry Grove Estates sell for?

MLS® records show 12 sales in the 12 months to early October 2026. Ten of the 12 sold between $150,000 and $225,000, with one below that range and one at about $250,000. Condition, additions, lot position and recent updates drive the difference. Pat can pull current comparables for any home you are considering.
