Neighbourhood Guide — Hunters Hill, Summerland

New luxury homes
above Okanagan Lake.

A newer hillside neighbourhood above Okanagan Lake with 12 listings at $290K–$2.95M, including homes at $2.6M–$2.95M and lots.

Listings describe Hunters Hill as a newer Summerland neighbourhood with net-zero-ready architectural homes by named builders, panoramic lake, vineyard and mountain views, 154-foot-wide lots and lots for sale. Several homes are new or under construction. One listing says the area is exempt from the foreign buyer ban; that is a regulatory claim that should be verified before anyone relies on it.

What Buyers Need to Know

Buying in Hunters Hill — the important details

⛰️ Hillside Sites

Slope, retaining walls, drainage and access matter on hillside lots. Ask for geotechnical and engineering documents.

🔥 Wildfire & Insurance

Hillside and interface properties can face tighter insurance. Get quotes before removing conditions.

🛡️ New-Home Warranty

Ask what warranty coverage applies and for how long.

🌍 Foreign Buyer Rules

One listing makes a claim about the foreign buyer ban. Verify any such claim yourself before relying on it.
