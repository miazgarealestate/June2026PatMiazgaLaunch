Frequently Asked Questions

Browse by Lifestyle — quick answers

What counts as luxury?

A $1.5M+ home that is waterfront, on half an acre or more, view, golf or estate-setting, built 1990 or later or renovated. Ultra-luxury is $3M and up. Land-first farms are listed separately.

Can I see actual listings?

This site shows aggregates. Pat can send current listings that match your criteria.

Do categories overlap?

Yes. A lakefront estate can be luxury, waterfront and view at once.
