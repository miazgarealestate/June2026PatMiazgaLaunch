Community Spotlight — Mount Baldy, Oliver BC

A small ski hill above wine country.
Lots, cabins and a condo.

A small ski-resort community above Oliver with 7 building lots from $75,000, a condo and ski cabins listed per MLS® listings.

Mount Baldy is described in MLS® listings as a family ski area with one of the highest base elevations in the Okanagan, about 40 full-time residents, and a village where everything is walkable. Eleven listings are active: seven building lots ($75,000–$129,900), a one-bedroom-plus-loft condo ($173,900) and three cabins ($450,000–$709,900). Some lots are on freehold land and some are in a bare land strata; this matters for fees.

Who Mount Baldy Is For

Buyers wanting a ski-in cabin or an inexpensive building lot

Mount Baldy suits buyers who want a ski cabin or an affordable recreational lot, with year-round use such as hiking and mountain biking described in listings.

Listings describe water, power and sewer at the lot line on some lots and no required building timelines; others sit in a bare land strata or on freehold land with no strata fee. Confirm which applies.

Several listings make claims about short-term rentals and taxes. Treat those as listing statements and verify with the RDOS and an accountant.

What Buyers Need to Know

Buying at Mount Baldy — the important details

🔌 Services

Confirm water, power and sewer availability and connection costs for the lot.

📄 Strata vs Freehold

Some lots are in a bare land strata; one is freehold with no strata fee. Confirm fees, bylaws and what is shared.

🏠 Short-Term Rental Rules

Do not rely on listing claims. Confirm zoning and rental rules.

🛣️ Access & Winter

Check road access and winter maintenance.

🔥 Wildfire & Insurance

Alpine and interface areas can be hard to insure. Ask early.

📄 BC Depreciation Report Rule

Since July 1, 2024, a strata corporation with five or more lots (bare land stratas included) can no longer defer a depreciation report by a ¾ vote. Outside Metro Vancouver, the Fraser Valley and the Capital Regional District, a strata with no report, or only one dated before December 31, 2020, has until July 1, 2027 to obtain one.
