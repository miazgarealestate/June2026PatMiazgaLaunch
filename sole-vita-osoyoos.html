Community Spotlight — Sole Vita, Osoyoos BC

Gated.
Low-density. Lakefront.

One of Osoyoos' most sought-after gated townhome complexes — low-density, close to the lake, with one-level living and genuine greenspace between units rather than a tightly packed layout.

Sole Vita, at 7200 Cottonwood Drive, has built a reputation among local listings as a particularly desirable gated lakefront community, distinguished specifically by its low density and the amount of greenspace between homes — a contrast to more tightly built strata developments elsewhere in Osoyoos. Units run one-level, two-bedroom, two-bathroom townhomes close to outdoor pools and the lake.

Who Sole Vita Is For

Buyers who want space between neighbours, not just a lake view

Sole Vita suits buyers for whom density matters as much as location. Many Osoyoos strata communities pack units close together to maximize lake frontage per building — Sole Vita's low-density layout with real greenspace between homes is a specific point of differentiation that repeat local listings consistently highlight as the property's main selling feature.

One-level townhome living removes stairs from daily life, which matters for retirement-stage buyers or anyone planning ahead for long-term mobility. Being close to the lake and outdoor pools without being directly on top of a high-traffic beach gives a quieter day-to-day feel than some of the busier resort-style communities covered elsewhere in this series.

As with any gated strata community, the gate and shared landscaping come with a strata fee — worth confirming exactly what it covers before assuming lower density automatically means lower monthly cost.
