Community Spotlight — The Villas, Osoyoos BC

Desert architecture.
A legal suite built in.

The Villas is a Mediterranean/desert-inspired townhome community at 8000 Vedette Drive, along Peanut Lake — walking distance to downtown Osoyoos and Gyro Beach, with many units including a fully self-contained legal suite.

Built starting around 2022, The Villas offers 2- and 3-storey townhomes with double garages, private decks, and — on many floor plans — a separate 1-bedroom legal suite with its own entrance, kitchen, bathroom, patio, and parking. Some units back directly onto Peanut Lake with water views; all residents share access to a private pool club with a hot tub and lounge area. Osoyoos Golf Club and several award-winning wineries are about a 5-minute drive away.

Who The Villas Is For

Central location, income potential, and desert-modern design

The Villas suits buyers who want new-ish construction without leaving downtown behind — few Osoyoos communities combine this level of walkability with legal-suite income potential. The Mediterranean/desert architectural style stands out from the area's more traditional builds, and the Peanut Lake setting gives even non-waterfront units a genuine water-adjacent feel.

The legal suite feature is a real differentiator: many units include a fully self-contained 1-bedroom suite with its own entrance, kitchen, bathroom, patio, and dedicated parking. That's a meaningful mortgage-helper or extended-family option that most Osoyoos townhome communities simply don't offer.

Being built starting around 2022, most units here are still on their first or second ownership — treat any purchase with the same due diligence as recent construction generally, even though it's not presale.

What Buyers Need to Know

Buying at The Villas — the important details

🔑 Legal Suite Registration

Confirm the suite on any specific unit is properly registered as a legal secondary suite with the Town of Osoyoos, not just marketed as one — this affects financing, insurance, and rental legality.

📜 Strata Documents

Review the strata's financial statements, meeting minutes, and depreciation report before offering — standard due diligence made more important given the community's youth.

🌊 Waterfront vs. Non-Waterfront

Not all units back onto Peanut Lake — pricing varies meaningfully between water-view and standard units. Confirm exactly what you're buying before comparing prices across listings.

🏊 Pool Club Costs

Shared amenities like the pool club typically carry their own cost allocation within strata fees — understand what's included and what maintenance reserve exists for it.

💵 GST on Recent Resales

Some recent listings note "GST included" — confirm whether GST applies to your specific purchase and whether it's built into the asking price or additional.

🔍 Pat's Assessment

Pat's construction background is useful for assessing build quality on this relatively young community, especially around the legal suite construction and waterproofing details.
