Estates, estate lots
and estate communities.

66 built estates and 36 estate-grade lots and acreages are listed across the South Okanagan, priced $265K–$19.5M, in the October 9, 2026 MLS® export.

Estates here means two things: luxury-grade homes on an acre or more, or described as an estate, gated or executive property; and building lots or acreages of up to about 15 acres, priced $250K or more, whose listings pitch a custom or dream-home site, a gated or estate community or a premium view. The named communities below are subdivisions where buyers can often choose between a finished home and a lot. The categories are keyword-based and approximate.

What this category means

Buyers who want a polished, higher-end neighbourhood, either a finished estate or a lot to build on

An estate lot is a design project. Review design guidelines, build deadlines, servicing costs and builder rules before you buy.

A built estate buys you time but not certainty; ask for construction documents, inspection reports and warranty history.

Estate neighbourhoods differ a lot in feel, from gated bare-land strata to open bench subdivisions. Pat can help you compare them.

What Buyers Need to Know

What buyers should know

📜 Design Guidelines & Covenants

Ask for the covenants, architectural guidelines, minimum sizes and build deadlines that apply to a lot.

🔌 Servicing & Build Cost

Confirm services at the lot line and get a realistic total build estimate. Pat's project-management background helps.

🏘️ Strata or Freehold

Bare-land strata estates have shared costs and bylaws. Freehold estates have their own rules. Know which you are buying.

💰 Closing Costs

Property transfer tax and other closing costs rise with price. See the BC Tax Guide and plan for them.

Frequently Asked Questions

Estates & estate lots — quick answers

How many estates & estate lots listings are there?

The Oct 9, 2026 MLS® export shows 102 that match, priced $265K–$19.5M. Inventory changes daily.

What is an estate lot?

In this site's counts, a building lot or acreage of up to about 15 acres, priced $250K or more, whose listing describes a custom-home, gated, estate or premium view setting.

Are all of these in named communities?

No. Some are on single rural parcels. The communities below are the named subdivisions with their own pages.
