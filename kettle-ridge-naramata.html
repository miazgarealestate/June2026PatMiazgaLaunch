Community Spotlight — Kettle Ridge, Naramata BC

Lakeview lots with community septic.
At the top of Arawana Road.

Lakeview building lots on Workman Place and Kettle Ridge Way, listed $265,000–$379,600 per MLS® listings, connected to a community septic corporation.

Kettle Ridge Lakeview Estates sits above the KVR Trail at the top of Arawana Road, minutes from Naramata Village per MLS® listings. Five building lots are active, $265,000–$379,600, from about a quarter to over half an acre. One listing says power is at the lot line and the lot can connect to the community septic corporation. A completed 2021 home is also listed at $1.4999 million.

Who Kettle Ridge Is For

Buyers who want to build on the Naramata Bench

Kettle Ridge suits buyers who want a lakeview lot with their own builder, near the KVR Trail and minutes from the village.

A community septic corporation means shared infrastructure and fees. Ask for the corporation's financials, rules and any levies.

Lots vary in how easy they are to build on; one listing calls its lot "stable and easy to build on." Verify with your own site review.

📄 BC Depreciation Report Rule

Since July 1, 2024, a strata corporation with five or more lots (bare land stratas included) can no longer defer a depreciation report by a ¾ vote. Outside Metro Vancouver, the Fraser Valley and the Capital Regional District, a strata with no report, or only one dated before December 31, 2020, has until July 1, 2027 to obtain one.
