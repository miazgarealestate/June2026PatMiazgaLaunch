About Casa del Sol

Townhomes and condos on 89th Street

Casa del Sol is a strata complex on 89th Street in Osoyoos. Listings describe a mix of townhome and condo units, typically 3 bedrooms and 2 to 3 bathrooms at roughly 1,300–1,400 sq ft, with main living on the ground floor in some units.

The buildings are three-storey stucco with terracotta-coloured tile roofs, arched window details and small balconies, set around lawns and planted beds.

One listing states that storage is located within the designated parking stalls and another mentions covered parking. A covered carport is visible beside the building, and several windows have wall-mounted air conditioners. Confirm what parking and storage come with the unit you are looking at.

Not to be confused with Casitas del Sol, a separate Osoyoos community.

What Buyers Need to Know

Buying at Casa del Sol — the important details

📄 New BC Depreciation Report Rule

As of 2024, deferring a depreciation report by vote is no longer allowed — South Okanagan strata corporations have until July 1, 2027 to be compliant if there is no report or the latest predates December 31, 2020. See the full guide →

📄 Strata Documents

Review the bylaws, minutes, depreciation report and reserve fund status. Ask about any special levy or major repair planned.

🏗️ Building Envelope

MLS® data shows the building was built in 2009. Ask the strata about the condition of the roof, windows and stucco, and for any repair history. Pat's construction background applies directly to judging these.

❄️ Air Conditioning

Many windows have wall-mounted units. Ask whether the strata has rules on installation, and who is responsible for the wall openings.

🅿️ Parking & Storage

A listing says storage sits within the designated parking stalls. Confirm which stall and storage space belong to your unit and whether they are common or limited common property.
