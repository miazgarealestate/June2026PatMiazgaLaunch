Community Spotlight — Sonora Gardens, Osoyoos BC

Detached homes
around a shared creek and pond.

Sonora Gardens is the name MLS® listings use for a neighbourhood of mostly single-level homes on Wildflower Court and part of Lobelia Drive in Osoyoos. Listings say it is not a strata: owners hold their own lot and share ownership of common grounds.

Listings describe a private park, waterfall, creek, putting green, pond and fruit trees, with homes built between about 2003 and 2006. A short walk to Haynes Point is mentioned in a listing. How the common property is shared is something to confirm before you buy.

How Sonora Gardens Is Structured

Freehold homes with shared common property

Listings for Wildflower Court and Lobelia Drive describe detached homes, each on its own lot of roughly 4,800–6,500 sq ft, set around landscaped common grounds. Listings say owners share the common property and describe it as not a strata.

Listings do not set out how the shared ownership works, what it costs or what rules apply. Ask for the documents early and have a BC real estate lawyer review them.

Not every home is fully standalone. Two Lobelia Drive listings (3200 and 3208) describe a “half-duplex style” home, so confirm how each lot is divided and what is shared with a neighbour.

What Buyers Need to Know

Buying in Sonora Gardens — the important details

📜 Shared Ownership

Ask for the title and any agreements or documents that set out how the common property is shared. Have a BC real estate lawyer review them before you remove subjects.

💵 Common-Area Costs

Ask whether there are fees or charges for the shared grounds, what they cover, and how they are set.

💧 Water Features

A pond, creek and waterfall need ongoing maintenance. Ask who looks after them and what rules apply.

🏘️ Half-Duplex Style Homes

Some listings describe half-duplex style homes. Confirm lot lines, any shared wall, and who insures and repairs what.

🏗️ Homes From 2003–2006

Ask about the age and condition of the roof, furnace, air conditioning, windows and stucco. One recent listing mentions a 2024 furnace and air conditioner. Pat's construction background helps here.

Frequently Asked Questions

Sonora Gardens — quick answers

What is Sonora Gardens in Osoyoos?

Sonora Gardens is the name MLS® listings use for a neighbourhood of detached homes on Wildflower Court and part of Lobelia Drive in Osoyoos, BC. Homes were built from about 2003 to 2006, and listings describe shared grounds with a creek, waterfall, pond and putting green.

Is Sonora Gardens a strata?

MLS® listings describe it as not a strata, with owners sharing the common property. Ask for the title and any governing documents to confirm how it works.

How much do homes in Sonora Gardens cost?

Four homes sold in the past 24 months from $725,000 to $815,000 (median $772,000; one was a court-ordered as-is sale), and two are listed at $799,900 and $849,000 (MLS® data to October 5, 2026).

What should I check before buying in Sonora Gardens?

Get the documents that cover the shared common property, including how it is owned, any costs and the rules, and who maintains the pond, waterfall and creek. Have a BC real estate lawyer review them before you remove subjects.
