Neighbourhood Guide — Grandview Terraces, Penticton

Modern luxury,
steps from the KVR Trail.

A luxury development by Agave Homes on Westminster Avenue East in Penticton, with 4 listings at $1.2M–$4.25M including homes at $3.98M–$4.25M and a building lot.

Listings describe Grandview Terraces as new California-modern homes by Agave Homes with reverse floor plans, elevators, lake and mountain views, outdoor kitchens and walking distance to downtown restaurants, breweries, the marina and the Farmer's Market. One listing says the builder has completed more than a dozen luxury homes in the area. Some homes are under construction; a building lot is also offered.

About this neighbourhood

Buyers wanting a new, architecturally designed luxury home near downtown Penticton

Grandview Terraces suits buyers who want a contemporary, low-maintenance luxury home with views and walkable access to downtown.

Because some homes are under construction, completion dates, specifications and deposit terms matter.

A building lot is also listed, which may suit buyers who want to work with the builder on a custom plan.

What Buyers Need to Know

Buying in Grandview Terraces — the important details

🏗️ Under Construction

Ask for the completion date, specification list, deposit protection and what happens if completion slips.

🛡️ Warranty

Ask what new-home warranty coverage applies.

🧾 GST

New homes can be subject to GST. Confirm treatment and what is included in the price.

💰 Closing Costs

Property transfer tax and other closing costs rise with price. See the BC Tax Guide and plan for them.
